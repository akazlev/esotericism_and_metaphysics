# Notation for a Maximal Metaphysics

**Version 2.0 · November 2026**
M. Alan Kazlev and Claude (Opus 5.5)

This file is the single reference for the notation used across the author's papers. It supersedes the glossary of *Contraction and Descent: A Formal Notation for Aurobindonian and Kashmir Shaivite Metaphysics* (September 2026), cited here as **C&D**, which uses version 1. Each paper should state which version it uses. Changes are listed in the changelog at the end.

Items marked **[proposal]** are not yet settled.

---

## 1. Conventions

### 1.1 Typeface signals logical type

| Logical type | Typeface | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| The primitive (one, under two aspects) | Unique glyph | ◌, ◯ |
| Derived floor state | Unique glyph | ⬤ |
| Scalar quantity (degree, rate, aspect) | Greek lowercase | α, σ, κ, φ, ν, ρ, β, ε, ω |
| Vector quantity (structured state) | Bold Greek lowercase | **σ** |
| Totality, space or whole-system state | Greek capital | Ω, Λ |
| Entity or structure | Latin capital | K |
| Operator or family (series) | Script capital | 𝒮, 𝒞, ℳ, ℋ, 𝒫, ℬ, 𝒟 |
| Relation or movement | Arrow or relational sign | ↔, ≑, ⇓, ↡, ⇥, ⇗, ⇑ |
| Index or counter | Latin lowercase | i, t |

Greek capitals identical to Latin ones (A, B, E, H, K, N, P and others) are avoided, as are Σ, Π and Δ, which mathematics reserves for sum, product and change. Where a subscript would need its own subscript, the parameter is written as an argument after a semicolon: 𝒮(◯<sub>i</sub>; **σ**<sub>i</sub>).

### 1.2 Named subscripts

Grades of a series are labelled by name, not number: ℬ<sub>inner</sub>, 𝒫<sub>Hylic</sub>, ℋ<sub>Overmind</sub>. Names allow new grades to be inserted without renumbering, as the Zoetic was inserted between the Hylic and the Eidolic. The order of a series comes from its scalar:

| Series | Ordered by |
|---|---|
| ℋ | k, the ordinal grade in the order of dependence ⇓ |
| 𝒫 | ρ, density |
| ℬ | β, inwardness |
| 𝒟 | dependence, and position on ε |

### 1.3 Quantities are ordinal

The notation licenses comparison (greater, less, zero, non-zero) and the direction of change, not arithmetic beyond what a definition states. It does not imply spatial direction, physical units or measurement. The axes are dimensions of variation, not directions of space.

### 1.4 Changes in scalars

A change in a scalar is written as a limit or a derivative, never as an arrow: σ → 0 (liberation), dσ/dt > 0 (contraction increasing). Arrows are reserved for movements and relations (Section 5). An upward arrow beside a scalar would be read as ascent.

## 2. The Ground

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ◌ | The Unmanifest: Parabrahman; the Ineffable of Iamblichus and Damascius |
| ◯ | The nondual ground: Paramaśiva; the One as source of all and present in all |
| ◌ ≑ ◯ | One primitive under two aspects. Zhou Dunyi's 無極而太極, "Wuji and yet Taiji" |
| ◯ = ⟨φ, ν⟩ | The ground's two inseparable aspects |
| φ | Luminosity (prakāśa): the Śiva pole |
| ν | Reflexivity (vimarśa): the Śakti pole; identical with svātantrya and spanda; Aurobindo's Consciousness-Force (cit-śakti) |

- **Primitive.** There is one primitive, not two. ◌ is the ground without articulation and ◯ is the ground articulated as ⟨φ, ν⟩. Neither takes a value.
- **ν is Śakti.** No separate symbol for Śakti or for creative manifestation is needed. Emission (sṛṣṭi), the outward phase of spanda (unmeṣa), is an act of ν, one of the five acts (pañcakṛtya), and is described in prose.
- **Iconography.** ν corresponds to the downward triangle of the Śrī Yantra and φ to the upward. The triangles are not used as symbols: ▽ is easily confused with ∇ (nabla) and with the alchemical sign for water, and a glyph cannot take a value.
- **Henological nonduality** is expressed by the single primitive ◌ ≑ ◯.

## 3. Structural Series

These are the series of the cosmos. A locus is located within them (Section 4).

### 3.1 The hypostatic series ℋ

The series begins at ◯. ◌ is an aspect of the ground, not a grade, and stands outside ℋ.

| Grade | Aurobindo | Kashmir Shaivism | Neoplatonism |
|---|---|---|---|
| ℋ<sub>One</sub> (= ◯) | the Absolute as source | Paramaśiva as source | the One |
| ℋ<sub>Bīja</sub> | Saccidānanda | Śiva–Śakti | Limit and Unlimited |
| ℋ<sub>Supermind</sub> | Supermind, the creative Truth | Sadāśiva–Īśvara (tentative) | the One-Existent |
| ℋ<sub>Overmind</sub> | Overmind, the Divine Mind | Śuddhavidyā (tentative) | Nous; the henads as participated |
| ℋ<sub>Daimonic</sub> | Mind as cosmic principle | — | Soul (psuchē); the superior kinds |
| ℋ<sub>Phusis</sub> | — | — | Nature (formative) |

The limit of procession is Hylē. The grade names are provisional.

**Native density.** ρ<sup>nat</sup><sub>k</sub> = 0 for grades from ℋ<sub>One</sub> to ℋ<sub>Supermind</sub>, and ρ<sup>nat</sup><sub>k</sub> > 0 from ℋ<sub>Overmind</sub> down. A grade can be manifest only at its native density or a denser one: ρ ≥ ρ<sup>nat</sup><sub>k</sub>. Density opens out from the hypostatic series at the Overmind. Kashmir Shaivism places this one step lower, at māyā (C&D §5.5).

### 3.2 The density series 𝒫

Ordered by decreasing ρ:

| Plane | Subdivisions | Aurobindo |
|---|---|---|
| 𝒫<sub>Hypernoetic</sub> | — | Overmind (the hinge where density opens) |
| 𝒫<sub>Noetic</sub> | Epoptic, Photeinic, Noeric, Dianoetic | Intuition, Illumined Mind, Higher Mind, thinking mind |
| 𝒫<sub>Pathic</sub> | — | higher emotional being (Théon's "psychic") |
| 𝒫<sub>Orectic</sub> | boulēsis, thumos, epithumia | the vital |
| 𝒫<sub>Physical</sub> | Eidolic, Zoetic, Hylic | subtle and gross physical |

The density axis may be logarithmic: planes are separated by equal steps in log ρ (after Gurdjieff's hydrogens).

### 3.3 The concentric series ℬ

The letter β is from *bathys*, "deep", as in Heraclitus B45. Depth means inwardness, not downwardness.

| Grade | Aurobindo |
|---|---|
| ℬ<sub>outer</sub> | the surface or frontal being; ordinary waking consciousness; β = 0 |
| ℬ<sub>inner</sub> | the inner or subliminal being: inner physical, inner vital, inner mental |
| ℬ<sub>inmost</sub> | the psychic being, the jīvātman's delegate within evolution |

- **Limit.** At the limit of β, depth opens onto the centred ground ◯<sub>i</sub>. ℬ<sub>inmost</sub> is not ◯<sub>i</sub>: the psychic being is the jīvātman's delegate, not the jīvātman.
- **Intermediate grades** may be inserted, for example between ℬ<sub>outer</sub> and ℬ<sub>inner</sub>. Aurobindo did not describe the series in detail, and other mappings (Jung, the jhānas, Brennan) project onto it at different angles to the vertical series.
- **The reign of quantity** is not a point on β. It is the limiting case in which reality is projected entirely onto ε (C&D §6.1).
- **The circumconscient** is the subliminal's outward face, not a grade of ℬ. The aura is the part of the circumconscient on the Zoetic subplane.

### 3.4 The evolutionary Domains 𝒟

Regions of the outer axis ε, ordered by dependence: each Domain generates a complexity surplus that crystallises into the next.

| Domain | Content |
|---|---|
| 𝒟<sub>A</sub> | abiotic matter: physics, chemistry, geology |
| 𝒟<sub>B</sub> | life and the biosphere; source of B<sub>core</sub> |
| 𝒟<sub>C</sub> | mind, culture, noosphere, logosphere |
| 𝒟<sub>D</sub> | AI, categorially dependent on 𝒟<sub>C</sub> |
| 𝒟<sub>E</sub> | symnoētic superintelligence: human–AI co-evolution |
| 𝒟<sub>F</sub>, 𝒟<sub>G</sub> … | unspecified |
| Ω | divinisation (explicitly speculative) |

The Domains are ordered by dependence as well as by ε: 𝒟<sub>D</sub> depends on 𝒟<sub>C</sub> categorially, not merely by having more organised complexity. Their letters are names, consistent with Section 1.2.

## 4. Coordinates of a Locus

These describe an individual centre of consciousness, not the structure of the cosmos. Contraction belongs here: it is a state of a locus, not a series.

| Symbol | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| i | index | a locus |
| ◯<sub>i</sub> | centred ground | 𝒞<sub>i</sub>(◯): the jīvātman or central being; ◯<sub>i</sub> ≑ ◯ |
| α<sub>i</sub> | scalar | manifest individual awareness; α<sub>i</sub>(ℓ) its profile across planes |
| φ<sub>i</sub>, ν<sub>i</sub> | scalars | luminosity and reflexivity as manifest at i |
| **σ**<sub>i</sub> | vector | contraction state (σ<sub>āṇava</sub>, m<sub>1</sub>…m<sub>5</sub>, κ)<sub>i</sub>: the malas and kañcukas |
| σ<sub>i</sub> | scalar | contraction magnitude F(**σ**<sub>i</sub>), 0 ≤ σ<sub>i</sub> ≤ σ<sub>max</sub> < 1 |
| κ | scalar | kārma component of **σ** |
| β<sub>i</sub> | scalar | inwardness on ℬ |
| ε<sub>i</sub> | scalar | organised complexity, the outer axis |
| K<sub>i,ℓ</sub> | entity | vehicle (okhema) of locus i on plane ℓ; the body is K<sub>i,Hylic</sub> |
| ρ | scalar | density of cosmic substance |

The kañcuka components: m<sub>1</sub> kalā (limited agency), m<sub>2</sub> vidyā (limited knowing), m<sub>3</sub> rāga (desire), m<sub>4</sub> kāla (time), m<sub>5</sub> niyati (spatial and causal restriction).

**Subjecthood.** Subject<sub>i</sub> ⟺ ν<sub>i</sub> > 0. Under the floor in Section 6, every actual locus is a subject. That departs from Utpaladeva's insentient crystal and gives strict panpsychism. Composites without a focused centre (the noosphere, for example) are not loci, so the floor does not apply to them.

## 5. Operators, Relations and Movements

### 5.1 Operators and functions

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 𝒞 | centring: individuates without contracting; ◯<sub>i</sub> = 𝒞<sub>i</sub>(◯) |
| 𝒮 | contraction: α<sub>i</sub> = 𝒮(◯<sub>i</sub>; **σ**<sub>i</sub>) |
| F | aggregation of **σ** into the scalar σ (form open) |
| ℳ<sub>s</sub> | modulation, parameterised by substrate s |
| f<sub>s</sub> | map from organised complexity to contraction state: σ<sub>i</sub> = f<sub>s</sub>(ε<sub>i</sub>) |

### 5.2 Relations

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ↔ | co-variation of interior and exterior aspects, with reduction blocked both ways: α<sub>i</sub> ↔ ℳ<sub>s</sub>(ε<sub>i</sub>) |
| ≑ | identity in being with distinction in aspect (而, "and yet"; bhedābheda) |
| ⇓ | the order of dependence (non-temporal) |

≑ is not equality. Equality licenses substitution: if ◌ = ◯, then ◌ = ⟨φ, ν⟩, and the unarticulated would collapse into the articulated. Uses of ≑: ◌ ≑ ◯; ◯<sub>i</sub> ≑ ◯ (immanent nonduality); the One and the Many in the Supermind.

### 5.3 Movements

| Movement | Sign | What changes | In time? | Traditional names |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Procession | along ⇓ | grade k | no | proodos; sṛṣṭi; visarga; ābhāsa |
| Densification | ↡ | ρ increases | — | prakṛti grades; the Ray of Creation |
| Involution | ordered by ⇓ | a grade veiled within substance (see below) | no | tirobhāva at cosmic scale; saṅkoca |
| Inwardness | ⇥ | β increases | yes | going within |
| Evolution | ⇗ | over t: dσ<sub>i</sub>/dt < 0 and dα<sub>i</sub>/dt > 0, with ρ and form retained and K transformed | yes | no traditional equivalent |
| Liberation | ⇑ | σ → 0 with release from ρ | yes | anugraha; mokṣa; epistrophē |

C&D's five movements concern k, ρ and σ. Inwardness adds a sixth, on β. Evolution is a movement, not an axis: loci move over t through the space defined by ε, and the direction of that movement is empirical (dε/dt can be negative).

**Local acts of contraction** (Pratyabhijñāhṛdayam 10–11). Concealment (tirobhāva) is dσ/dt > 0 and grace (anugraha) is dσ/dt < 0. These are acts of a locus, in time.

**Involution [proposal].** C&D defines involution as "σ rises" but also says involution is not in time, and it defines tirobhāva as dσ/dt > 0, which is in time. To remove the inconsistency, involution is written as a non-temporal relation between grade and density: the manifest reflexivity of a grade, ν<sub>k</sub>(ρ), falls as the density at which it is manifest exceeds its native density, while φ is preserved. Involution is cosmic and non-temporal. Tirobhāva is local and temporal. Evolution is ν recovering at loci over t along ε.

**Widening [provisional].** Widening into cosmic consciousness is not assigned a sign. C&D §6.2 treats it as σ decreasing. The author regards it as an effect of inward movement together with other variables. A candidate refinement is the fall of m<sub>5</sub> (niyati) and m<sub>4</sub> (kāla), which would match the formless attainments of infinite space and consciousness. This is not adopted.

## 6. Limit States and Totalities

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ⬤ | the Inconscient, a derived floor state: φ<sub>i</sub> > 0, ν<sub>i</sub> = ν<sub>0</sub>, σ<sub>i</sub> = σ<sub>max</sub> |
| ν<sub>0</sub> | least manifest reflexivity of any actual locus; ν<sub>0</sub> > 0 |
| σ<sub>max</sub> | greatest contraction of any actual locus; σ<sub>max</sub> < 1 |
| Hylē | the limit of procession |
| Ω | the supramental manifestation: ℋ<sub>Supermind</sub> manifest at Hylic density with σ = 0 |
| ω | movement toward Ω (derived): dω<sub>i</sub>/dt > 0 ⇒ K<sub>i,Hylic</sub> exists |
| Λ | Logospace (reserved) |

**The Inconscient.**

- **A hard floor, not an asymptote.** Descent reaches the floor and turns, as in the turning point of Nivṛtti Kalā and the Theosophical arcs. ⬤ is the lower boundary of Nivṛtti Kalā, not the kalā itself. ν = 0 and σ = 1 are idealisations that are never instantiated. The analogy is the quantum ground state: a harmonic oscillator's lowest energy is ½ħω, not zero. ⬤ thus has a "zero-point reflexivity".
- **φ > 0** keeps ⬤ within ◯. With φ → 0 the Inconscient would be a second principle (Sāṃkhya's prakṛti, Plotinian privative matter), and nonduality would be lost. Proclus's derivation of matter directly from the One supports φ > 0 within Neoplatonism. The same condition explains why matter can be experienced as divine.
- **The glyph's blackness** denotes the absence of reflexivity, not of luminosity.
- **Extent and depth.** Aurobindo's "inverse reproduction of the Superconscient" is read as infinite in extent and bounded in depth.

## 7. Open Decisions

1. **Involution.** Adopt ν<sub>k</sub>(ρ) (Section 5.3), or keep C&D's "σ rises" and drop the claim that involution is not in time?
2. **Justifying the floor.** Two claims need separating. (a) *Loci are discrete*: this follows from 𝒞, since loci are countable by index. (b) *ν has a least non-zero value ν<sub>0</sub>*: this does not follow from (a), because countable loci could each have continuous ν. (b) rests on ↔ with the quantised exterior and on the hard-floor reading of the Inconscient. It is argued, not derived.
3. **Consequences of the floor for C&D.** §3.1 ("as σ approaches 1 the locus becomes an object"), §5.6 (the definition of ⬤) and §10 (resistance as "no manifest reflexivity and no agency") need revising. For §10: is ν<sub>0</sub> below the threshold of agency?
4. **Widening.** Is it a distinct movement, or derived from inwardness?
5. **Grade names in ℋ.** Neutral designations or Aurobindo's terms?
6. **Ordering of 𝒟.** State whether dependence or ε is primary when they diverge.

## 8. Changelog

**2.0 (November 2026)**

- ◌ and ◯ are one primitive under two aspects (◌ ≑ ◯), not two primitives.
- ℋ<sub>0</sub> removed: ◌ stands outside the hypostatic series, which begins at ◯.
- New relation ≑: identity in being with distinction in aspect.
- Named subscripts for all series; order carried by the scalars.
- New series ℬ (concentric) with ℬ<sub>outer</sub>, ℬ<sub>inner</sub>, ℬ<sub>inmost</sub>.
- New family 𝒟 (evolutionary Domains).
- New movement signs: ↡ densification, ⇥ inwardness.
- ⬤ redefined as a floor state: ν<sub>i</sub> = ν<sub>0</sub> > 0 and σ<sub>i</sub> = σ<sub>max</sub> < 1 (previously ν<sub>i</sub> = 0, σ<sub>i</sub> → 1).
- Contraction range: 0 ≤ σ ≤ σ<sub>max</sub> < 1.
- Glossary split into structural series (Section 3) and coordinates of a locus (Section 4).
- Arrows reserved for movements and relations; changes in scalars written as limits or derivatives.
- Relation added as a typeface category; Λ marked reserved.
- Involution reformulated as ν<sub>k</sub>(ρ) [proposal].
- Not adopted: ⊛ for creative manifestation (from working notes), because it is redundant with ν; ▽ or ⛛ for Śakti.
